About the Festival

A Celebration That Gives Back

The Festival of the Arts is VAH's flagship event, bringing together performers from around the world to celebrate creativity while raising funds for humanitarian causes. Artists of all ages and disciplines are invited to participate.

🌍

Global Participation

Performers join from dozens of countries, creating a mosaic of cultural expression that transcends borders.

🎭

All Disciplines Welcome

Dance, music, theatre, spoken word, and fusion arts — if you create, there is a stage here for you.

❤️

Humanitarian Impact

Every ticket and sponsorship is channelled into vetted humanitarian initiatives around the world.

How It Works

Festival Structure

Each festival spans multiple days of programming across three pillars: performances, workshops, and cross-cultural collaboration.

🎬

Performances

  • Dance Showcases
  • Music Performances
  • Theatre & Spoken Word
  • Fusion & Cross-Cultural Acts
  • Opening & Closing Galas
🎓

Workshops

  • Dance Workshops
  • Music Workshops
  • Career Development Sessions
  • Youth Artist Labs
  • Collaborative Jams
📅

Format

  • Multiple Days of Programming
  • Virtual & In-Person Options
  • Live-Streamed Performances
  • On-Demand Access
  • Networking Receptions

For Artists

How to Participate

Artists may submit applications to perform. Applications may be virtual or in-person depending on the festival edition. All disciplines and experience levels are encouraged.

1

Performance Video

Submit a recent recording of your work — live footage preferred, 3–5 minutes ideal.

2

Short Artist Bio

Introduce yourself: your background, discipline, and what drives your creative practice.

3

Technical Requirements

Detail any stage, sound, lighting, or streaming needs so we can match you to the right setup.

Ready to take the stage?

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. We welcome artists of all experience levels, genres, and nationalities.
